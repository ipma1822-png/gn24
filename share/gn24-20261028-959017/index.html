Texas Taekwondo Master Rafael Fabian Lopez Burgoa Completes 127th Kukkiwon International Master Course in New Jersey

Experienced martial arts educator reports earning Second-Class International Taekwondo Master certification following professional training organized by Kukkiwon World Taekwondo Academy
Global News24 편집부 · Global News24

[GLOBAL NEWS24 | WOODBRIDGE, NEW JERSEY, USA]

Rafael Fabian Lopez Burgoa, a Texas-based taekwondo master and martial arts educator, has reported completing the 127th International Taekwondo Master Qualification Course organized by Kukkiwon World Taekwondo Academy (WTA).

The professional training program was held from August 28 to 31, 2026, at APA Hotel Woodbridge in Woodbridge, New Jersey, United States.

According to information submitted to GLOBAL NEWS24, Burgoa participated in the international training program as part of his continuing professional development.

He also reported obtaining Second-Class International Taekwondo Master certification through the program.

■ PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT THROUGH KUKKIWON TRAINING

Kukkiwon World Taekwondo Academy provides educational programs designed to strengthen the professional knowledge and teaching abilities of taekwondo instructors.

International master training emphasizes the importance of technical knowledge, educational responsibility, leadership, and the development of qualified instructors.

Taekwondo masters are responsible not only for teaching martial arts techniques but also for creating positive learning environments and supporting students' physical and personal development.

By participating in the 127th International Taekwondo Master Qualification Course, Burgoa continued his commitment to improving his teaching expertise and understanding of international taekwondo education.

■ EXPERIENCED TAEKWONDO INSTRUCTOR AND MARTIAL ARTS EDUCATOR

Burgoa reports holding a sixth-degree black belt from Kukkiwon and an eighth-degree rank from the World Taekwondo Han Moo Kwan Federation.

He also states that he holds an International Poom-Dan Examiner qualification from Kukkiwon.

With extensive experience in taekwondo training, instruction, athlete development, and martial arts education, Burgoa has devoted much of his professional career to promoting technical excellence and educational values.

He is associated with TAAG Royal Martial Arts Burgoa and the Royal Academy of Martial Arts Science USA.

Based in Texas, he continues to support martial arts instruction and the development of future generations of practitioners.

■ EDUCATION BEYOND TECHNICAL SKILLS

Burgoa's teaching philosophy places importance on the relationship between technical development and personal character.

Taekwondo is widely recognized as a Korean martial art that combines physical training with values such as courtesy, integrity, perseverance, self-control, and respect.

For children and young adults, martial arts education can provide opportunities to develop confidence, responsibility, leadership, and teamwork.

Burgoa emphasizes the importance of helping students apply the lessons learned through taekwondo to their everyday lives.

This approach reflects the educational role of martial arts instructors in supporting students both inside and outside the training environment.

■ CONTINUING COMMITMENT TO INTERNATIONAL STANDARDS

Professional development remains an important responsibility for martial arts educators working in an increasingly international environment.

Participation in recognized training programs allows instructors to expand their knowledge, strengthen teaching practices, and engage with broader professional communities.

Burgoa described his participation in the 127th International Taekwondo Master Qualification Course as an important milestone in his continuing development as an instructor.

His involvement reflects a commitment to lifelong learning and the advancement of taekwondo education.

■ SUPPORTING THE GLOBAL DEVELOPMENT OF TAEKWONDO

Originating in Korea, taekwondo has expanded into a global martial art practiced by millions of people.

International instructors play an important role in sharing its techniques, traditions, and educational values with students around the world.

The professional development of overseas taekwondo masters contributes to maintaining educational standards and strengthening international cooperation.

Burgoa's participation in the Kukkiwon training program illustrates the continued efforts of instructors to advance their professional qualifications and serve the global taekwondo community.
