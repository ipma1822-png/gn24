Springboks Prepare for Australia Test as South Africa’s 2026 Rugby Campaign Continues

South Africa turns attention to the Wallabies in Perth following a demanding international season
2026-09-26 · Global News24 편집부 · Global News24

PERTH — South Africa’s Springboks are preparing for another major international test as they face Australia in Perth on Sunday, 27 September.

The match comes during a demanding 2026 campaign that has taken South Africa through fixtures against several leading rugby nations and a high-profile four-Test series against New Zealand.

South Africa and New Zealand renewed one of international rugby’s longest-running rivalries in a special four-Test programme during August and September, with matches staged in South Africa and the United States.

Attention has now shifted to Australia.

Springbok preparations in Australia have included opportunities for players seeking to strengthen their positions within an increasingly competitive national squad.

The 2026 season is also part of a broader process of building squad depth ahead of the 2027 Rugby World Cup in Australia, where South Africa will attempt to defend the title it won in France in 2023.

Rugby occupies a distinctive place in South African sport. The Springboks’ international performances attract audiences well beyond traditional rugby communities and major matches regularly become national sporting events.

South African rugby is simultaneously working to maintain elite international performance while developing younger players capable of competing at Test level.

The Perth match therefore represents more than another fixture on the calendar. It provides another opportunity to test combinations and squad depth against high-level opposition as preparations for the next Rugby World Cup cycle continue.

Following the Australian encounter, South Africa’s international programme will continue later in the year with further fixtures in the northern hemisphere.
