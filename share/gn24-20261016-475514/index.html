USA Martial Arts Team Prepares for 2026 Pan American Club Championships in Florida

DAYTONA BEACH, FLORIDA, USA — A team of four promising young martial artists from TAAG Royal Martial Arts – Burgoa is preparing to participate in the 2026 Pan American Club Championships, scheduled to take place from October 22 to 25, 2026, in Daytona Beach, Florida.

The team, associated with Houston, Texas, will participate under the leadership of Grandmasters Fabian Burgoa and Fabiola Burgoa, who have dedicated their efforts to developing young athletes through martial arts education and competitive training.

FOUR YOUNG ATHLETES PREPARE FOR INTERNATIONAL COMPETITION

The participating athletes are:

• Dayyin Rauff

• José Hernández

• Eva Niño

• Duane Madison

According to information shared by Grandmaster Fabian Burgoa, these young competitors have demonstrated commitment, discipline, and determination throughout their training.

Their participation in the upcoming championship represents an important opportunity to gain international competitive experience while demonstrating their skills and sportsmanship.

DEDICATED LEADERSHIP AND MARTIAL ARTS EDUCATION

Grandmasters Fabian Burgoa and Fabiola Burgoa have been guiding and preparing the athletes for this international event.

Their coaching emphasizes not only technical performance but also respect, perseverance, confidence, and personal development.

Through their continued commitment to martial arts education, the instructors seek to help young athletes develop the character and discipline necessary for success both inside and outside the competition arena.

PAN AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP BRINGS ATHLETES TOGETHER

The 2026 Pan American Club Championships will provide an international competitive setting for participating martial arts clubs and athletes.

For the TAAG Royal Martial Arts – Burgoa team, the event offers an opportunity to test their abilities, gain valuable experience, and establish connections within the wider international taekwondo community.

The athletes are expected to approach the competition with pride, determination, and respect for their fellow competitors.

CONNECTING AMERICAN MARTIAL ARTS WITH THE INTERNATIONAL COMMUNITY

Grandmaster Fabian Burgoa has also expressed his interest in strengthening cooperation with the International Police Martial Arts Alliance (IPMA) and developing broader connections among martial arts instructors and organizations throughout the United States.

His willingness to share information about the upcoming championship represents a positive step toward greater international communication and cooperation.

GLOBAL NEWS24 welcomes opportunities to introduce meaningful martial arts activities, youth development programs, educational initiatives, and international sporting events from the United States to its international readership.

SUPPORTING THE NEXT GENERATION

The participation of these four young athletes highlights the importance of providing opportunities for the next generation to develop confidence, discipline, and international experience through martial arts.

Regardless of the final competition results, preparing for an international championship can provide valuable lessons in dedication, teamwork, perseverance, and personal growth.

GLOBAL NEWS24 extends its best wishes to Grandmasters Fabian Burgoa and Fabiola Burgoa, as well as athletes Dayyin Rauff, José Hernández, Eva Niño, and Duane Madison, for a successful and rewarding championship experience.
