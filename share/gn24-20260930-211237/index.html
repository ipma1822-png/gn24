Grand Master Fabián Burgoa Expands Martial Arts Leadership from Houston

Houston martial arts leader participates in Oklahoma championship and prepares a community self-defense seminar in Houston for November 7
Global News24 편집부 · Global News24

HOUSTON, TEXAS — Grand Master Fabián Burgoa, an 8th Dan martial artist based in Houston, Texas, is continuing to expand his martial arts activities through competitions, seminars and community-based self-defense programs.

Burgoa recently participated in the 34th Annual Oklahoma US Central Open Taekwondo Championships, held in Oklahoma on September 26, 2026. According to Burgoa, the event was led by Great Grandmaster In Hui Won.

Burgoa is the founder and representative of TAAG Royal Martial Arts Burgoa in Houston, Texas, and serves as a U.S. representative of the Global Police Martial Arts Alliance.

He is also President of the Houston Branch of the International Police Martial Arts Association (IPMA), maintaining international communication and martial arts exchange with IPMA headquarters in Korea.

Following his recent activities in Oklahoma, Burgoa is preparing for his next community program in Houston.

A community self-defense seminar titled “Seminario Comunitario de Defensa Personal” is scheduled to take place in Houston, Texas, on November 7.

The seminar will be led by Grand Master Fabián Burgoa and Master William Burgoa, a 5th Dan martial artist.

The program is expected to focus on practical self-defense training for the community while promoting martial arts education, safety awareness and continued exchange among martial arts practitioners.

Burgoa has also expressed his intention to continue sharing photographs, videos and information from his future martial arts activities with GLOBAL NEWS24.

Through these records, GLOBAL NEWS24 USA EDITION will continue to document martial arts activities taking place in Houston and other parts of the United States, as well as international exchanges connected to martial arts, education and community safety.

Burgoa’s recent participation in Oklahoma and his upcoming November 7 seminar in Houston represent the continuation of his active martial arts work based in Texas.

GLOBAL NEWS24 USA EDITION will continue to follow his future activities and developments.
