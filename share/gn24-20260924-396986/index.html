GLOBAL NEWS24 INDIA EDITION Opens a New Window Connecting India, Korea and the World

The new country edition will bring stories of India’s culture, technology, education, sports, youth and international cooperation to a wider global audience.
2026-09-24 · Global News24 편집부 · Global News24

GLOBAL NEWS24 · INDIA EDITION

GLOBAL NEWS24 is expanding its international news network with the development of INDIA EDITION, a country-focused digital news edition designed to connect stories from India with readers around the world.

India is home to one of the world’s largest and most diverse societies, with a long history, rich cultural traditions and a rapidly changing contemporary landscape.

From historic heritage and regional cultures to technology, education, sports and the ambitions of younger generations, India offers a wide range of stories with international relevance.

GLOBAL NEWS24 INDIA EDITION aims to provide a new digital window through which these stories can reach a broader audience.

The edition will cover subjects including culture, education, science, technology, sports, martial arts, youth, cities and international cooperation.

One important area of interest will be the connection between India and the Republic of Korea.

Despite their geographic distance, the two countries provide many opportunities for people-to-people communication through culture, education, technology, business, sports and youth activities.

Digital communication is making such connections easier than ever.

Online education, international networks and digital media allow students, professionals, athletes and organizations in different countries to exchange knowledge and experiences without being limited entirely by distance.

Technology will also be an important subject for INDIA EDITION.

India has a large community of students, engineers, researchers, entrepreneurs and technology professionals participating in a rapidly evolving digital environment.

Artificial intelligence, digital services, future mobility, space science and emerging technologies are among the subjects that are increasingly shaping conversations about the future.

At the same time, India's identity cannot be understood through technology alone.

Its languages, arts, food, architecture, festivals, traditional practices and regional communities form an important part of the country's cultural diversity.

Sports provide another opportunity to connect India with the world.

Cricket has a highly visible place in Indian sporting culture, while many other sports and martial arts are practiced across the country.

International competitions, educational programs and digital events can give young athletes new opportunities to meet participants from other countries and broaden their experiences.

The younger generation will be an important focus of INDIA EDITION.

Education, digital skills, creativity, sports and international communication can help young people develop the knowledge and confidence needed to participate in an increasingly connected world.

GLOBAL NEWS24 also intends to highlight stories that encourage greater understanding between cultures.

International news does not need to focus only on governments or major institutions.

The experiences of students, teachers, athletes, researchers, artists, communities and ordinary citizens can also help people in different countries understand one another.

GLOBAL NEWS24 INDIA EDITION is a country news edition within the GLOBAL NEWS24 international network. It does not by itself represent the establishment of a legal branch or official office in India.

As the edition develops, more stories from India and its connections with Korea and the wider international community will be introduced.

Through a digital network linking different country editions, GLOBAL NEWS24 aims to create more opportunities for stories to cross borders and reach new audiences.
