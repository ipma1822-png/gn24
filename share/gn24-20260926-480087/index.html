Nigeria Begins AFCON 2027 Qualification Campaign with New Faces in Super Eagles Squad

Eric Chelle introduces fresh players as Nigeria turns its attention toward the next Africa Cup of Nations
2026-09-26 · Global News24 편집부 · Global News24

ABUJA — Nigeria’s Super Eagles have entered the qualification campaign for the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations with a squad combining established internationals and new players as head coach Eric Chelle continues to shape the national team.

Nigeria was drawn into a qualifying group with Madagascar, Tanzania and Guinea-Bissau, beginning another competitive cycle for one of African football’s most prominent national teams.

Chelle has used the latest international window to introduce new players to the senior squad, expanding the options available to Nigeria as qualification progresses.

Squad depth has already become important.

Star striker Victor Osimhen was ruled out of Nigeria’s opening AFCON 2027 qualifiers while continuing his recovery, creating an early test of the team’s attacking alternatives.

Nigeria’s football expectations remain high.

The Super Eagles have a long history in the Africa Cup of Nations and continue to draw players from leading domestic and international leagues.

The qualification campaign will also provide an opportunity for younger and newly selected players to establish themselves at senior international level.

For Nigerian supporters, international football remains one of the country’s most powerful shared sporting experiences, bringing together audiences across regions, languages and communities.

The Super Eagles’ performance is therefore followed far beyond the stadium.

Each qualification window becomes a test not only of individual talent but also of tactical consistency, squad depth and the ability to integrate players based across different leagues and football systems.

With the road to AFCON 2027 now under way, Nigeria will be seeking to build a stable squad capable of navigating qualification and competing at the highest level of African football.
