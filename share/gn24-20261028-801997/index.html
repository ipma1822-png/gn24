TAAG Royal Martial Arts Burgoa Athletes Win Four Golds and Two Silvers at 2026 Pan American Taekwondo Championships

Texas-based martial arts team celebrates international success under coaches Fabian and Fabiola Burgoa, with William Lopez contributing to athlete development and youth education
Global News24 편집부 · Global News24

[GLOBAL NEWS24 | DAYTONA BEACH, FLORIDA, USA]

TAAG Royal Martial Arts Burgoa, a martial arts organization based in Texas, has reported outstanding results at the 2026 Pan American taekwondo competitions held in Daytona Beach, Florida, in October 2026.

According to information submitted to GLOBAL NEWS24 by the organization, its athletes earned a total of four gold medals and two silver medals, demonstrating dedication, discipline, and competitive spirit on the international stage.

The team participated in competitions identified in its submitted materials as the Pan American Championships and Daytona International Tournament.

The results reflect the combined efforts of committed athletes, experienced coaches, and supportive families.

■ ATHLETES ACHIEVE IMPRESSIVE RESULTS

The team reported the following individual achievements:

Dayyin Rauff

First Place — Machine Division

Gold Medal

Eva Niño

Two First-Place Finishes

Two Gold Medals

Jose H.

First Place — Torch Division

Gold Medal

Duane Madison

Two Second-Place Finishes

Two Silver Medals

Together, these results represent four gold medals and two silver medals.

The athletes demonstrated their technical abilities and competitive determination while representing their team at an international event.

Participation in international competitions also provides young athletes with opportunities to develop confidence, gain experience, and interact with competitors from different backgrounds.

■ LEADERSHIP OF FABIAN AND FABIOLA BURGOA

Coaches Fabian Burgoa and Fabiola Burgoa played important roles in preparing and supporting the athletes throughout their competitive journey.

Fabian Burgoa is a martial arts instructor who reports holding a Kukkiwon sixth-degree black belt and an eighth-degree rank from the World Taekwondo Han Moo Kwan Federation.

His educational philosophy emphasizes technical excellence alongside discipline, respect, responsibility, and leadership.

Fabiola Burgoa has also contributed to athlete training, competition preparation, and team development.

Together, the coaches continue to encourage students to pursue excellence both in competition and in everyday life.

■ COACH WILLIAM LOPEZ CONTRIBUTES TO YOUTH DEVELOPMENT

William Lopez also participated in the Pan American Club Championships held in Daytona Beach from October 22 to 25, 2026.

His event credential identifies him as a Color Belt Coach and Associate Coach (Kyorugi).

In addition to his coaching responsibilities, Lopez has contributed to martial arts educational activities designed to help children develop discipline, respect, confidence, coordination, and positive personal values.

His involvement highlights the broader educational responsibilities of martial arts instructors beyond athletic competition.

For young students, martial arts training can support physical development while encouraging perseverance, teamwork, and responsible behavior.

■ SUCCESS BUILT ON TEAMWORK AND FAMILY SUPPORT

TAAG Royal Martial Arts Burgoa emphasizes three essential elements behind athletic success: dedicated coaches, hardworking athletes, and supportive families.

The organization believes that lasting achievements are built through consistent training, encouragement, discipline, and community involvement.

Every medal represents countless hours of preparation and the collective commitment of everyone involved in an athlete's development.

The team's philosophy recognizes that true champions are developed not only through victories but also through character, perseverance, and respect for others.

■ PROMOTING INTERNATIONAL TAEKWONDO DEVELOPMENT

The achievements of TAAG Royal Martial Arts Burgoa demonstrate the continued growth of taekwondo and martial arts education in the United States.

Originally developed in Korea, taekwondo has become an internationally practiced martial art and competitive sport.

Its educational values extend beyond competition, encouraging discipline, self-confidence, respect, and personal development.

Through international participation, coaches and athletes help strengthen connections among martial arts communities around the world.

GLOBAL NEWS24 will continue reporting on the achievements of martial arts athletes, instructors, and organizations contributing to international sports development and youth education.
